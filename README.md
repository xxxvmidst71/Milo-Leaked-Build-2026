## 🕹️ About

Milo is a first-person horror horde-survival game set in a hellish war zone crawling with deranged cultists. Fight alone or in co-op, feed corpses to your growing mischief of rats, collect survivors, and extract to Traang’s Arcade for tickets, upgrades, and stranger risks. This is a raw leaked pre-release build that surfaced before the planned Early Access launch.

For players who want grimy first-person gunplay, co-op survival, graphic gore, dark humor, and an unfinished build with sharp edges.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🔫 **Horde Survival** — Hold out against waves of cultists using a brutal World War-era arsenal.
- 🐀 **Rat Mischief** — Feed corpses, rescue rats, and grow your strange biological advantage.
- 🩸 **Corpse Popping** — Charge a bizarre ability by sending the dead back into the fight.
- 🤝 **Solo or Co-op** — Survive alone or fight alongside up to three other players.
- 🎰 **Traang Arcade** — Extract with your rats and spend tickets on upgrades through arcade games.
- ⚔️ **Three Alignments** — Choose Survivor, Loyalist, or Apostate classes with unique loadouts and abilities.
- 🧪 **Stackable Buffs** — Build each run around strange interactables, temporary boosts, and risky choices.
- 🏆 **Leaderboards** — Push deeper into the nightmare and compete for global scores.
